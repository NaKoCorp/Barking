# Contributing to Barking

Contributions are welcome. To report a vulnerability, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening an issue.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before your first pull request can be merged, you sign the
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). You do it once, by posting a comment
on the pull request when the bot asks for it.

In short:

- You keep the copyright on what you contribute.
- You allow the maintainer to distribute your contribution under other
  licenses than AGPL-3.0, including commercial ones. This is what makes it
  possible to fund the project by selling commercial licenses.
- In return, everything merged into the project stays available under
  AGPL-3.0 or another OSI-approved license.

Read [CLA.md](CLA.md) for the full terms. Signatures are public: they are
recorded on the `cla-signatures` branch.
